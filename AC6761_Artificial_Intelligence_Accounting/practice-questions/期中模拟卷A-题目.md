# AC6761 期中模拟卷 A · 试卷

本卷按现有课件和 Case 的能力要求原创，情境、数字、题序与分值由本卷设置。它不是往年真题、教师试卷或教师押题。

建议闭卷完成，限时 120 分钟，总分 100 分。全部题目必答。Part A 为非论述题，共 70 分；Part B 为论述题，共 30 分。建议 Part A 用 85 分钟，Part B 用 30 分钟，最后 5 分钟检查。

范围为 Week 1–5。Week 6 Access 操作、SQL 和后续 AI 专题不在本卷中。允许基本计算器属于本卷训练设置，不代表真实期中考试的工具规定。

题干用英文，附中文任务说明；建议用英文作答。中文说明仅帮助识别任务，不改变英文条件。所有金额为 HKD，除 Q4 明确使用 HKD thousand 外均为 HKD。忽略税、利息、折旧及未列出的交易。本卷两家服务企业均按 sole proprietorship（独资企业）处理。

本公开版仅含题目，不包含个人作答或答案解析。本卷不要求提交到 Canvas。

## Part A · Non-essay questions（70 marks）

### Q1 · Identify the accounting principle（8 marks；建议 7 分钟）

For each independent scenario, name the most directly relevant accounting principle or assumption and briefly explain its application. Each scenario carries 2 marks.

1. A shop purchased equipment for HKD18,000. A dealer now offers HKD23,000 for it. Under the historical-cost basis used in this question, the shop initially records the equipment at HKD18,000.
2. A company reports measurable monetary transactions, but does not add “employees are highly motivated” as a monetary amount in its accounts.
3. A sole proprietor pays a personal holiday bill using the business bank account. The payment is recorded as an owner withdrawal, rather than a business operating expense.
4. A firm buys equipment expected to be used for several years. It prepares accounts on the assumption that the business will continue operating, rather than immediately liquidating all assets.

中文任务：每个情境写最直接对应的原则／假设名称，再用一句话说明题面为什么符合它。不要只写英文名。

### Q2 · Accounting equation analysis（12 marks；建议 12 分钟）

SwiftLink, a newly established delivery service, has zero opening balances. Analyse the following six transactions in October. Supplies in transaction 3 have not yet been consumed.

1. The owner invested HKD16,000 cash.
2. The business purchased equipment for HKD5,000 cash.
3. The business purchased unused supplies for HKD800 on account.
4. It completed delivery services for HKD3,000 on account.
5. It collected HKD2,000 from the customer billed in transaction 4.
6. It paid HKD400 to the supplier in transaction 3.

Required: Use the three-column format `Assets | Liabilities | Equity`. Show the signed effects of every transaction and the cumulative balances immediately after it. Where one asset is exchanged for another, show both signed movements within the Assets cell. Each transaction and its balance row carry 2 marks.

中文任务：每笔先写变化，再写该笔后的累计余额。资产内部交换不能只写净变化 0；要展示一增一减。所有变化金额带正负号。这里要求会计等式分析，不要求分录。

### Q3 · Journal entries, T-accounts and trial balance（20 marks；建议 25 分钟）

Harbour Tutoring began business on 1 September with zero opening balances. It undertook these transactions:

1. Sep 1: The owner invested HKD30,000 cash.
2. Sep 1: Paid HKD6,000 cash for rent covering September, October and November equally.
3. Sep 2: Purchased equipment for HKD9,000 cash.
4. Sep 4: Purchased unused supplies for HKD1,800 on account.
5. Sep 10: Completed tutoring services for HKD6,000 cash.
6. Sep 15: Completed tutoring services for HKD4,000 on account.
7. Sep 20: Paid HKD500 for utilities consumed in September.
8. Sep 23: Paid HKD1,200 to the supplier in transaction 4.
9. Sep 25: The owner withdrew HKD1,000 cash for personal use.
10. Sep 28: Collected HKD2,500 from the customer in transaction 6.
11. Sep 30: Recorded the portion of prepaid rent used in September.
12. Sep 30: Supplies costing HKD1,200 were used during September and must be recorded as an expense.

Required:

(a) Prepare all 12 journal entries.（12 marks；每笔 1 分）

(b) Show both sides of every affected T-account, using transaction numbers, and determine every ending balance. You may use a table with columns `Account | Debit entries | Credit entries | Ending balance`.（4 marks）

(c) Prepare the adjusted trial balance at 30 September, before closing entries.（2 marks）

(d) Calculate September net income and ending owner's equity.（2 marks）

中文任务：写 12 笔分录、全部受影响账户的借贷明细与余额、调整后但尚未结账的试算表，最后算利润和期末权益。第 12 笔的 1,200 是“已经耗用的金额”，不是剩余存货。

### Q4 · Common-size balance sheets（12 marks；建议 13 分钟）

Below are simplified balance sheets for two fictional companies as at 31 December 2025. Both use the same accounting standards. Amounts are in HKD thousand; the two columns are companies, not years.

| Item | CloudBridge | MetalWorks |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cash and cash equivalents | 2,400 | 1,000 |
| Accounts receivable | 1,800 | 3,000 |
| Inventories | 600 | 6,000 |
| Property, plant and equipment, net | 1,200 | 8,000 |
| Intangible assets | 6,000 | 2,000 |
| Total assets | 12,000 | 20,000 |
| Current liabilities | 1,800 | 5,000 |
| Long-term debt | 1,200 | 7,000 |
| Owner/shareholder equity | 9,000 | 8,000 |
| Total liabilities and equity | 12,000 | 20,000 |

The liability categories shown are complete. No further liabilities exist for this question.

(a) Convert the five asset line items and the three financing line items into percentages of each company's own total assets. Round to one decimal place. Do not treat total rows as additional components.（8 marks；16 个百分比各 0.5 分）

(b) Calculate total liabilities as a percentage of total assets for each company.（2 marks）

(c) Calculate MetalWorks' PPE percentage minus CloudBridge's PPE percentage. State the result in percentage points.（2 marks）

中文任务：分别用本公司资产总额作分母。计算 16 个共同比、两家总负债占比及 PPE 占比差。表中数字单位为千港元，百分比计算时分子分母用相同单位。

### Q5 · Multiplicities and relational tables（8 marks；建议 11 分钟）

(a) A supplier may be registered before any purchase occurs. Every completed purchase has exactly one supplier. A supplier may supply many purchases. State both endpoint multiplicities of the `Supplier–Purchase` association.（2 marks）

(b) A purchase may have no purchase return or several separate return events. Each return event must relate to at least one purchase and may combine goods from several purchases. State both endpoint multiplicities of `Purchase–PurchaseReturn`. Count purchase events and return events, not batches or item quantities.（2 marks）

(c) `CustomerID` is the declared primary key of Customer. The current data are shown below. Sale S9 contains `CustomerID=C99`, but C99 does not exist in Customer. Identify the two integrity problems.（2 marks）

| CustomerID | CustomerName |
|---|---|
| C01 | Ada |
| （null） | Ben |
| C02 | Ada |

(d) Cashier K1 is Mei; K2 is Leon; K3 is Amy. Receipt R1 for HKD300 is handled by K1; R2 for HKD200 by K2; R3 for HKD500 by K1. Each receipt must have exactly one cashier, and a cashier may have no receipts. Create the two relational tables, show all six records, and mark the primary and foreign keys.（2 marks）

中文任务：前两问写两端数字并解释固定哪个对象数哪个对象；第三问识别两种完整性错误；最后把收款和收银员转成表。两位 Ada 的编号不同，题目没有说她们是同一人。

### Q6 · Revenue-process REA model（10 marks；建议 17 分钟）

WaveStore sells individually numbered surfboards. Each board may be sold at most once; returns and resale are outside this question. A sale contains one or more boards. Unsold boards can already be registered.

Credit sales, partial payments and combined payments are allowed. No customer advance payments or overpayments are allowed. Cash may also be received from a bank loan; loan receipts have no Customer link and no Sale link in this revenue-process model.

Each sale has exactly one customer and one salesperson. Each customer-related cash receipt has exactly one customer; the same customer may make multiple receipts. Each receipt is deposited into exactly one cash account and handled by exactly one cashier. Cash accounts, customers and cashiers may be registered before any transaction.

Every customer is assigned to exactly one salesperson. Every registered salesperson must already be assigned to at least one customer and may be assigned to several. Salespersons may exist without any sales. Customer assignment is independent of who handles an individual sale; do not assume the two salesperson identities must be the same.

Required:

(a) Identify the seven classes and classify them as Resources, Events or Agents.（2 marks）

(b) Draw the eight associations, naming their types and showing both endpoint multiplicities. `Assignment` must be included. A clearly labelled association table is acceptable for this mock.（4 marks）

(c) Sale S1 to customer C1 is HKD1,200; Sale S2 to the same customer is HKD800. Receipt R1 of HKD900 is applied as HKD700 to S1 and HKD200 to S2. Receipt R2 of HKD500 is applied only to S1. Receipt R3 of HKD400 is from a bank loan. List all sale–receipt allocation records and calculate C1's remaining receivable.（2 marks）

(d) Place each of these five attributes on the correct class or association: `Sale total amount`, `Cash receipt total amount`, `Cash balance`, `Customer account receivable`, `Cash receipt amount applied to sale`.（2 marks）

中文任务：资源、事件、参与者共 7 类；画 8 条关联并标 16 个端点。只需写第 (d) 问给出的属性。第 (c) 问所有金额均为本卷原创；不能把一笔合并收款的全额重复记给每笔销售。

## Part B · Essay questions（30 marks）

### Q7 · Explain cash, profit and trial-balance limits（10 marks；建议 10 分钟）

Use the Harbour Tutoring facts in Q3. Explain the reasoning, not just debit/credit directions.

(a) Why is the HKD6,000 rent payment initially an asset, and why is September rent expense only HKD2,000? Show the September effect on prepaid rent, cash and equity.（4 marks）

(b) Why do collecting HKD2,500 from an existing receivable and withdrawing HKD1,000 by the owner not change September net income? State their different effects on assets and equity.（3 marks）

(c) Suppose transaction 6 was completely omitted: neither debit nor credit was recorded. Would the trial balance still balance? Quantify the effect on receivables and net income.（3 marks）

中文任务：解释付款和耗用为何分开；回款和收入为何分开；业主提取和费用为何分开。最后用漏记赊销说明“借贷相等”能查什么、查不出什么。

### Q8 · Interpret financial-statement structure（10 marks；建议 10 分钟）

Using Q4, write a short comparative analysis supported by numbers.

(a) Which company has a structure more consistent with an asset-light technology business, and which with a manufacturing business? Explain using PPE, inventories and intangible assets. These are structural clues, not verified descriptions of actual businesses.（5 marks）

(b) Compare the two financing structures using total-liability and equity percentages. Explain one risk consideration without assuming all liabilities are interest-bearing loans.（3 marks）

(c) Can these balance sheets alone establish which company is more profitable or will definitely repay its debts? Explain two information limitations.（2 marks）

中文任务：用比例支持推断，再说明推断边界。不能凭资产大、现金多或负债少直接断言利润更高。

### Q9 · Explain REA and business-event types（10 marks；建议 10 分钟）

(a) Explain how a REA model and a double-entry system describe the same business from different perspectives. Give two concrete information needs that an event-centred database can support, and one limitation of adopting REA. Do not claim that ordinary accounting software is incapable of keeping supplementary data.（4 marks）

(b) Use Q6 to explain `Stockflow`, `Duality` and `Participation`, with one correct association for each. Explain why `Assignment` cannot be replaced by a salesperson's participation in a sale.（3 marks）

(c) Distinguish a purchase order, receipt of purchased goods, and purchase return as business-event types. State when the inventory resource changes, and explain why a purchase order does not by itself prove the goods have already been received.（3 marks）

中文任务：先说业务对象与动作，再给术语。第 (c) 问假设购货的所有权在企业收到货时转移；不涉及预付款、在途货或其他特殊条款。
