## 0. 译法约定（本课统一一次，之后不再讨论）

会计术语的中英对应比哲学术语稳定，但**港式、内地、台湾三地译法有实质差异**。本课**统一采用内地简体译法作为正文用词**，并在下表注明另外两地的说法。

**为什么选内地译法**：读者的中文语感是简体内地的；而**考试全部用英文作答**，中文只是理解的脚手架，不进考卷。所以选"读起来最不费劲"的那一套即可。**但港式译法必须知道**——本课在香港上课，教授的口语、本地年报（HKFRS 口径）、以及同学的表达都可能用港式词。

**其他约定**

1. **首次出现写 `中文（English Term）`**，之后视上下文用中文或英文。
2. **考试关键定义保留英文原句** —— 考卷用英文写。
3. **不译的词**：GAAP、IFRS、FASB、IASB、SOX、XBRL、MD&A、REA、SEC、PCAOB 一律保留英文缩写。
4. **`unqualified` 的翻译陷阱**：`unqualified opinion` = **无保留意见**（**最好**的审计意见），不是"不合格意见"。`qualified opinion` = **保留意见**（有问题）。中文语感与英文完全相反，必须记牢。

It is a book amount, not automatically the market value of the company.


**营业周期**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The time for a firm to convert cash back into cash (purchase material, manufacture product, sell product, and collect cash).


**流动资产**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Under the IAS 1 context used here, meeting any applicable criterion makes an asset current.

The criteria include realisation, sale or consumption in the normal operating cycle; trading purpose; or expected realisation within twelve months.

Cash or cash equivalents are also current, unless restricted from exchange or use to settle a liability for at least twelve months after the reporting period.

现金及现金等价物通常为流动；若兑换或用于清偿负债的限制持续到报告期后至少 12 个月，则不适用这一现金条件。其他条件及例外见 §2.2.2。


**非流动资产**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Assets that do not meet the applicable current-asset criteria.

Ease of sale alone does not determine the classification.


**物业、厂房及设备**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Tangible assets held for production, supply, rental or administration and expected to be used for more than one period.

Subsequent carrying amounts depend on the applicable cost or revaluation model, depreciation and impairment; land and other non-depreciable components need separate consideration.


**折旧**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The systematic allocation of an asset's depreciable amount, after estimated residual value, over its useful life.

Depreciation itself is a non-cash allocation; the original purchase may have required cash.


**摊销**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The systematic allocation of the depreciable amount of a finite-life intangible asset over its useful life.

Indefinite-life intangible assets and acquisition goodwill do not follow this annual amortisation rule in the IFRS context explained here.


**账面价值**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The amount at which an asset is recognised after applicable accumulated depreciation or amortisation and impairment adjustments.

Cost less those adjustments is the cost-model example, not the only measurement basis.


**公允价值**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The price that would be received to sell an asset in an orderly transaction between market participants at the measurement date.


**无形资产**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：An identifiable non-monetary asset without physical substance.

Identifiability may arise from separability or contractual/legal rights; acquired goodwill is considered separately.


**外购商誉**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：A residual recognised in a business combination after identifying and measuring the acquired assets and assumed liabilities.

In this section's simplified 100% acquisition with no previous holding or other adjustments, goodwill equals consideration less the fair value of identifiable net assets.


**自创商誉**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Internally generated goodwill is not recognised as an asset.

This does not mean that every internally developed intangible must be expensed: recognition of other items depends on their own applicable criteria and must be assessed separately.


**存货**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Inventories are assets held for sale, in the process of production for sale, or materials or supplies to be consumed in production or in rendering services.

They are measured at the **lower of cost and net realisable value**. Delaying a required write-down or recording fictitious inventory can overstate assets and profit.


**净变现价值**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Estimated selling price less the estimated costs of completion and of sale.


**减值**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：A reduction in an asset's carrying amount under the applicable measurement rule.

Inventories use cost versus NRV, IAS 36 non-financial assets use recoverable amount, and receivables use expected credit losses.


**盈余管理**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Using estimates, methods or timing choices to influence reported earnings.

Reasonable estimates are necessary; knowingly delaying a required impairment or recording fictitious inventory may be an error, material misstatement or fraud rather than a permitted choice.


**现金及现金等价物**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Cash is cash on hand and demand deposits.

Cash equivalents are short-term, highly liquid investments held for short-term cash commitments, readily convertible to known amounts of cash and subject to an insignificant risk of changes in value; the usual three-month guide runs from acquisition to maturity.


**预期信用损失**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Expected credit loss (ECL) is a probability-weighted estimate of credit losses that uses reasonable and supportable information about past events, current conditions and forecasts.

A loss allowance reduces the net carrying amount of applicable receivables.


**一年内到期的长期负债**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The current portion of long-term debt is the part of long-term financing that meets any current-liability criterion, for example because it is due within twelve months after the reporting period.

A liability is also current if the entity has no reporting-date right to defer settlement for at least twelve months.


**应计费用**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Liabilities for goods or services already received, or expenses already incurred, that have not yet been paid, invoiced or formally agreed; the amount may require a reasonable estimate under the accrual basis.


**递延收入**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Consideration received or due before the entity transfers the promised goods or services; it is presented as a **contract liability** until the related performance obligation is satisfied.


**应付债券**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Debt raised by issuing bonds to investors under specified contractual terms.

Bonds may be publicly offered or privately placed; transferability, maturity and issue size depend on the arrangement.


**优先股**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：In the simplified equity-classified, non-participating case, preference shares carry a fixed dividend preference and rank ahead of ordinary shares in distributions and liquidation, often with limited voting rights.

Classification depends on contractual cash and redemption obligations, not the legal label alone.


**股本溢价**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Under a par-value share regime, share premium is the amount subscribers pay above par or stated value.

A no-par regime may credit the full issue proceeds to share capital instead.


**库存股**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The entity's own equity instruments repurchased and held without cancellation; under IAS 32 they are generally deducted from equity.

Whether shares may be held or cancelled also depends on the applicable legal and contractual setting.


**其他综合收益累计额**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：A cumulative equity balance at the reporting date for amounts recognised in other comprehensive income under the applicable rules.

Current-period OCI is a flow; AOCI is the accumulated balance.

Whether an amount is later reclassified to profit or loss depends on the item and category.


**已实现 / 未实现**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：In this securities example, realized means the security has been sold, while unrealized means it remains held despite a value change.

This status alone does not determine whether the change goes to profit or loss or OCI.


**少数股东权益**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Equity in a controlled subsidiary that is not attributable, directly or indirectly, to the parent.

It is a real ownership interest rather than a balancing plug, and its share of profit and OCI is attributed separately.


**表外项目**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Resources, obligations or risks not recognised as line items on the balance sheet.

Whether they are disclosed in the notes depends on the applicable standard, materiality and facts; remote contingent liabilities are generally not disclosed under IAS 37.


**使用权资产**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：An asset representing a lessee's right to use an underlying leased asset during the lease term.

IFRS 16 requires a lessee to recognise the right-of-use asset and the related lease liability under its general model.

The A-company 2019 presentation is compatible with a lease-accounting transition, but the specific bridge still requires Note 15; a general effective date alone does not prove this company's balance movement. 🔗 [IFRS 16 official page](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-16-leases/), accessed 2026-10-25.


**联营公司**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Investees over which the investor has **significant influence**, after first excluding control or joint control.

A holding of 20% or more of voting power creates a rebuttable presumption, not a hard 20%–50% rule; associates are generally accounted for using the equity method.


**共同比分析**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Converts line items into percentages of **total sales** for an income statement or **total assets** for a balance sheet, making structure easier to compare while retaining the need to inspect absolute size, accounting policies, periods and underlying risks.


**百分比变动分析**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：$(\text{current period}-\text{prior period})\div\text{prior period}\times100\%$; expresses the change relative to the prior-period amount as a percentage, without removing scale, currency, accounting-policy or base-value limitations.


**毛利**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：$\text{Revenue}-\text{Cost of revenue}$; reflects the ability to generate profits from revenue.


**销货成本**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The cost attributable to the goods or services sold; for manufactured inventory it can include direct materials, direct labour and allocated production overhead.


**营业利润**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：In this lecture's format, gross profit plus other income and gains, less distribution, administration and other expenses. The actual subtotal depends on the entity's applicable presentation.


**财务费用**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Costs incurred in maintaining debt finances (bank loans, etc.).


**每股收益**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Basic EPS divides profit attributable to ordinary equity holders of the parent by weighted-average ordinary shares outstanding.

Diluted EPS adjusts both numerator and denominator for dilutive potential ordinary shares and excludes anti-dilutive instruments. See §2.5.2 for the example and limits.


**利得 / 损失**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The lecture's labels for results typically arising from non-operating or incidental activities; in the broader conceptual framework they are categories within income or expenses, not separate financial-statement elements.


**经常性 / 非经常性项目**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Items assessed by whether their nature, frequency and magnitude represent an ongoing pattern.

Recurring items can inform forecasts after adjustment; nonrecurring items should be explained separately rather than automatically deleted.


**盈余质量**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The extent to which reported earnings are supported by verifiable activity, neutral estimates, understandable disclosures, repeatable sources and explainable cash conversion.


**直接法**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Reports major classes of gross operating cash receipts and gross operating cash payments, such as cash collected from customers and cash paid to suppliers and employees.

It is a method of presenting CFO, not a different method for CFI or CFF.


**间接法**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Reconciles the applicable profit subtotal to CFO by adjusting non-cash items, operating deferrals and accruals, and profit-or-loss items whose cash effects are investing or financing.

The lecture's “almost all firms” wording is historical and does not establish a current universal usage rate.


**经营活动现金流**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Cash flows from the entity's principal revenue-producing activities and other activities that are not investing or financing.

Positive CFO is evidence of operating cash generation, not automatic proof of profitability or earnings quality.


**投资活动现金流**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Cash flows from acquiring and disposing of long-term assets and investments not included in cash equivalents, subject to the applicable trading/dealing scope.

A negative net amount means investing outflows exceeded inflows; it does not by itself prove expansion or value creation.


**筹资活动现金流**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Cash flows that change the size or composition of contributed equity and borrowings, such as issuing shares, obtaining or repaying borrowings, and payments to owners.

The sign shows net financing direction, not whether financing is good or bad.


**缴入资本**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The lecture's contributed-capital grouping includes common stock, equity-classified preferred stock, additional paid-in capital and treasury stock; actual columns depend on the applicable capital regime and transactions.


**赚得资本**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：The lecture groups retained earnings and accumulated other comprehensive income as earned capital. This grouping does not make the balance cash received or automatically distributable.


**报表附注**

笔记整理的考试英文表达（非逐字讲义原句）：Notes are an integral part of the financial statements and provide applicable details, accounting policies, estimates and risks.

The governing disclosure requirements and note numbering depend on the reporting framework, issuer and jurisdiction; notes do not guarantee an answer to every business or legal question.
