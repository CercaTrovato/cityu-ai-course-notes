### 原有演讲者备注

We use normalized common-share EPS multiplied by an assumed earnings multiple as a transparent sensitivity. The numbers on this slide are analyst assumptions, not company forecasts or consensus estimates. Monster's central value is forty-four dollars, around six percent below the reference price. These choices are debatable, which is exactly why they are visible. The downside and upside ranges are not confidence intervals and have no assigned probabilities. Different sustainable-earnings assumptions can change both recommendations.

### 原有演讲者备注

Our ratings are HOLD for both companies, but the reasoning differs. Monster is our stronger business-quality choice. Its reference price nevertheless requires sustained earnings growth, so we do not automatically translate quality into a BUY. Celsius offers more portfolio optionality but also more uncertainty. Its central normalized EPS assumes a significant recovery from reported GAAP earnings, so a low multiple based on adjusted EPS cannot settle the debate. We would reconsider after a lower entry price or stronger sustainable results. Conversely, loss of margin durability at Monster, or continued original-brand weakness and poor cash conversion at Celsius, could support a SELL. A rating should change when its evidence or assumptions change, not when a presenter needs a more dramatic conclusion.

### 原有演讲者备注

AI assisted discovery, extraction, calculation code, qualitative coding and first-draft writing. We adopted outputs only where a source and definition supported them. For numerical work, the important unit is not just a number but a number with an issuer, period, currency, scale and accounting label. We did not conduct a multi-model benchmark or independent human-coder agreement test. The group still needs to review the evidence, debate the scenarios and make its own assessment before submission.

### 原有演讲者备注

Two practical source problems occurred during this work. A generically named annual-report PDF found through search belonged to Hayward rather than Celsius. Checking the issuer prevented that source from entering the analysis. Celsius's 2025 PDF also produced unreadable encoded text during local extraction. We reconciled the financial figures with the HTML filing and visually rendered statement pages. Accounting checks addressed different risks: consolidated net income is not income for common holders, preferred stock is not ordinary debt, and a stock split changes the EPS basis. These examples show that AI output can be plausible yet incompatible with the task. Automated arithmetic checks are useful, but they do not replace source identification or financial judgment.

### 原有演讲者备注

The final answer to our research question is that Monster currently shows the clearer conversion of growth into reported profitability and cash. Celsius has expanded its portfolio, but the acquisition effect, distributor transition and preferred-share claims complicate the benefit to ordinary investors. We have not treated those complications as proof that its strategy will fail. They are reasons to demand stronger evidence and a more cautious valuation. Our HOLD ratings reflect the stated prices and scenarios, not an attempt to avoid a conclusion. For discussion, consider which observation would change your view first: a lower entry price, stronger original-brand sales, a margin recovery or cash generation after settlements. We welcome challenges to both the interpretation and the assumptions.
