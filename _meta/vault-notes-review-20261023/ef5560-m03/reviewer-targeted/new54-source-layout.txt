Selection and Shrinkage
Forward Selection: Building a Short Predictor List



   Begin with an intercept-only model.


   1. Add each unused predictor in turn. Fit every candidate by OLS on the same training
      months.

   2. Compare validation MSE. Keep the best candidate only if it improves on the current model.

   3. Repeat with the remaining predictors. Stop when validation MSE no longer falls.


   The result is a short predictor list. Signals useful only together may be missed.
