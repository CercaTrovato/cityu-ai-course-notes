### 4. 【当堂练习 = 考点信号】业主提取 vs 股利，影响权益的哪一块

> **教授的口诀**（转录 `01:46:02`）：
> > *"just remember that… **paid in capital** — that is the increase for the capital stock or so-called common stock. And if they say **stock withdraw**, so that will **decrease capital stock**. And if they say there is a **revenue**, that is **increase retained earnings**; there is the **expense**, **decrease retained earnings**. **Dividends decrease retained** earnings. But **as long as you know which component will be affected — capital stock or the retained earnings — it's easy to finish the table.**"*

| 事项 | 股本 | 留存收益 |

### 5. 【概念】US GAAP 规则导向 vs IFRS 原则导向

**W2-5. 【口诀】收入、普通股与权益同向（贷增）；股利、费用与权益反向（借增）——教授重复了四次**

> **原话**（`01:11:40`–`01:12:09`，教授自己的记忆法）：*"equity means plus revenue, minus dividend and minus expense… the treatment for the common stock and the revenue would be the same as equity… the dividend [and] expense, because it's negative, it's [the] opposite way—the increase in dividends would be [a] debit, increasing expense [is a] debit."*

**含义**：借贷规则不用背整张表，记等式方位 + 权益四子项的正负号即可。对应：[[M02-交易的会计处理#2.4 借与贷：复式记账的核心|M02 §2.4]]

> **原话**（`53:30`）：*"**Every time you see 'in advance', you should be careful.** And the company has not [done the work] yet. So this money is unearned revenue… that is a liability."*
> **原话**（`52:53`，预付一年保险）：*"we didn't enjoy the insurance benefit yet… **it's not [an] expense account. It's a prepaid account. It's [an] asset.**"*

**含义**：交易 #12（预收 3,000）与 #13（预付保险 2,400）是这条规则的讲义版本。对应：M02 §2.2.2 ③、§2.6.3

**W2-7. 【题型】给一组交易，"tell me the ending balance for each account"**

> **原话**（`01:33:20`）：*"So that's why we have another case [two] here, chapter [2?] practice. **No need to submit.**"*；（`01:35:21`）*"**Tell me the ending balance for each account.**"*
