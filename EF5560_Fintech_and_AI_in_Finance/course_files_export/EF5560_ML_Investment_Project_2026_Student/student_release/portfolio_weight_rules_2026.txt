EF5560 2026 ML Investment Assignment
PORTFOLIO WEIGHT RULES

These rules apply to the final long-only weight CSV. They keep portfolios
fully invested and limit single-stock concentration for a common comparison.

1. PORTFOLIO CONSTRAINTS - EVERY WEEK

- Invest only in the 80 stocks in submission_template_2026.csv.
  Each stock must satisfy 0 <= weight <= 0.10: no short selling, maximum 10%.
- Sum of the 80 weights = 1.00 (100%). No cash allocation or borrowing.
  A stock you do not hold has weight zero; keep its column in the file.
- Rebalance weekly for Weeks 105-260: 156 weekly portfolios.
  Start from equal weights at the end of Week 104. Ignore transaction costs.

The 10% cap implies at least 10 stocks with positive weights each week.
There is no separate sector cap or turnover limit. In Q5, report the largest
weekly sector allocation during validation and discuss concentration risk.

2. CSV FORMAT

- Filename: EF5560_leadstudentid.csv, replacing leadstudentid with your ID.
- Exactly 156 data rows plus the header; 81 columns: forecast_date followed
  by the 80 ticker columns. Keep all column names and their original order.
- Keep the template's exact dates and row order: 2023-07-07 to 2026-06-26.
  These are simulation labels. Do not change them to match trading holidays.
- Use decimal weights with at least eight decimal places: 0.05000000 means
  5%, not 5 or 5%. Enter 0.00000000 for unheld stocks; leave no blanks.

Each row gives the weights held over the following week's return period.
Select the model/rule on validation data, refit on Weeks 1-104, and keep the
fitted model fixed when forming weights from each private week's predictors.

3. EXAMPLES FOR ONE WEEK

Valid: 16 stocks at 0.06250000 each; the other 64 at zero. Total = 1.00.
Valid: all 80 stocks at 0.01250000 each. Total = 1.00.
Invalid: 5 stocks at 0.20000000 each. Total = 1.00, but the 10% cap is exceeded.

The top-16 rule is required for the Q5 model comparison, not for your final
submitted strategy. Another final long-only rule is allowed if supported by
validation evidence and consistent with these constraints. Long-short results
belong in the report, not this CSV.

4. CHECK BEFORE SUBMISSION

Keep the official template and checker together. Run either command, using
your own filename:

  python check_submission_2026.py EF5560_12345678.csv
  Rscript check_submission_2026.R EF5560_12345678.csv

The checker requires finite numeric weights and uses an absolute rounding
tolerance of 1e-6 for the weight bounds and row sums. It does not rescale
weights. A pass confirms file format and weight constraints, not performance.
Invalid weight files receive zero private-performance points; report marks
are assessed separately. Submit this weight CSV with your report PDF and code
ZIP appendix, as specified in the assignment brief.
