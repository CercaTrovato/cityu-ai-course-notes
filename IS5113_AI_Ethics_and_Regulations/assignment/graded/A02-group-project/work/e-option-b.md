## 怎样接进现有组稿

- **第 11 页讲为什么需要第二个判断。** 一双鞋可以符合功能要求、造型也好看，但如果整批候选都沿用近似鞋身轮廓，设计团队仍可能漏掉其他做法。Nike 的 A.I.R. 特写描述早期生成图的流线风格相似。R2 数的是头像草图沿用了多少共同示例特征。这两份材料可用来提出问题，未测 Nike 最终鞋款的多样性。[Nike A.I.R. 特写](https://about.nike.com/en/magazine/nike-design-athlete-imagined-revolution) · [R2 作者稿](https://qiushi-zhou.github.io/PDF/CHI-2024-AI.pdf)
- **第 15 页把两个判断放进同一次设计审批。** 先逐款核设计目标、功能和来源。再把通过的候选摆在一起，比轮廓、结构和材料路线。若多个候选只换了颜色，评审者可以要求补一个结构上不同的备选，或记录保留共同风格的理由。若某张图与可识别来源明显相似，单独追查来源与权限。第 13–14 页的预测样本覆盖仍独立交代。
- **第 32 页用 R3 回答“怎么可能既更好又更像”。** 研究比较的是可使用 GPT-4 点子的写作条件和无此机会的条件。对一篇故事的评价与它同其他故事的相似度是两种测量。用这一点解释第 15 页的组内提案。[R3 arXiv v3，图 2、图 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00506v3)

## 可给统筹的英文报告段落

> Before approving a collection of AI-assisted designs, a brand should assess each concept against the design brief and then compare the shortlisted concepts as a set. A design can meet the brief even when most alternatives share the same silhouette or construction. In a short-story experiment, access to GPT-4 ideas improved some ratings of individual stories, while stories also showed greater within-group content similarity (Doshi & Hauser, 2024, arXiv v3, Figs. 2 and 5). The result motivates a separate collection-level review. If the shortlist varies mainly in colour, reviewers should request a structurally different alternative or document why a shared design direction serves the brief. Footwear teams would need to test how well this review works in practice.

第 15 页备注可用一句短版。

> Review each concept against the brief. Then compare the shortlist for meaningful differences in silhouette, construction and materials, and record the reason for keeping a common direction or requesting another option.

## 两条英文答问

**Can every design be good while the collection still lacks variety?** Yes. Each shoe could meet its brief and look polished, while the shortlist repeats one silhouette with different colours. We would review individual concepts against the brief and compare the shortlisted designs for meaningful variation.

**Does the writing study show that Nike’s footwear became less diverse?** The experiment measured eight-sentence stories. Writers offered GPT-4 ideas received higher ratings on some measures, and their stories were, on average, more similar to the other stories in their own group than stories in the no-access group were to theirs. We use this as a reason to check both outcomes in footwear design.
