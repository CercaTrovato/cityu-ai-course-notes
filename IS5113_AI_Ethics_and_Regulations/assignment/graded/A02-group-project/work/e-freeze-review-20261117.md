## 冻结前两项建议

**1. 第 8 页把证据范围写准确。**

当前主页面底部是 “No public source links Celect to A.I.R.; current workflow undisclosed.” 这句话覆盖所有公开来源，比我们实际完成的查证范围更宽。最前面的新版英文口播已采用“the public evidence we reviewed”的限定，可将页面与该口径统一。建议主页面改为：

> The sources reviewed do not establish a Celect–A.I.R. link; Nike’s current forecasting workflow remains unverified.

同页末尾 “K3 detail for Q&A” 还重复 “No public source links Celect to A.I.R.”，也一并改为：

> The sources reviewed do not establish a link between Celect and A.I.R.

这处调整只收窄查证范围，保留三类活动分别举证的原意。

**2. 第 10 页备注补 R3 的出处。**

本次逐字检索 10 页正文和备注，没有找到 Doshi、Hauser、R3 或 2312.00506 的引用。第 10 页却已经使用短篇故事实验做问答，建议在这条答问后加：

> [R3] Doshi, A. R., & Hauser, O. P. (2024, arXiv v3). Generative artificial intelligence enhances creativity but reduces the diversity of novel content. PDF pp.2–3, 4–6, 9–10; Figs. 2 and 5. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.00506v3  
> Scope: eight-sentence stories, AI-idea access, reader ratings and within-group content similarity. Transfer to footwear requires separate testing.
