Question 4. Based on the 4x4 grid plot in Section 2.5 of the tutorial (Moons, Blobs, Varied, Aniso), summarize which linkage method (Single, Average, Complete, or Ward's) is most suitable for which type of data shape, and state your final rule of thumb for selecting a linkage method. [20 points]¶

  • Moons: Single linkage (5 points) is most suitable because it can capture non-convex, connected, and curved structures (5 points).
  • Blobs: Ward’s linkage (5 points) works well for compact, spherical, similar-sized clusters because it tends to minimize within-cluster variance (5 points).
  • Varied: Average linkage (5 points) is suitable for clusters with different spreads and variances (different size), providing a balance between single and complete linkage (5 points). (or Ward’s linkage will also get points)
  • Aniso: Average linkage (5 points) can be suitable for elongated or anisotropic clusters with different spreads and variances (different size) (5 points). (or Complete linkage or Ward’s linkage or even Single linkage will also get points, since there is no perfect one for Aniso)
