## G · 五个可用理论（M02）

| 中文 | English | 英文定义（可默写） | 首现 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 定言令式（第一表述） | Categorical Imperative (1st formulation) | Act only from moral rules that you can, at the same time, will to be universal moral laws. | M02 §2.6.2 |
| 定言令式（第二表述） | Categorical Imperative (2nd formulation) | Act so that you treat both yourself and other people as ends in themselves, and never **only** as a means to an end. | M02 §2.6.4 |
| 假言令式 | Hypothetical Imperative | A conditional command that applies only if you want to achieve a certain outcome; not a moral law. | M02 §2.6.3 |
| 完全义务 | Perfect duty | A duty obliged to fulfil without exception; violating it leads to a logical contradiction if universalized. Example: telling the truth. | M02 §2.6.7 |
| 不完全义务 | Imperfect duty | A duty obliged to fulfil in general but not in every instance, allowing discretion in how and when. Example: helping others. | M02 §2.6.7 |
| 概念矛盾 | Contradiction in conception | A maxim that cannot even be conceived as a universal law because the concept destroys itself; generates perfect duties. | M02 §2.6.7 |
| 意愿矛盾 | Contradiction in the will | A maxim that could exist as a universal law but which no rational being could consistently will; generates imperfect duties. | M02 §2.6.7 |
| 效用 | Utility | The tendency of an object to produce happiness or prevent unhappiness for an individual or a community. | M02 §2.7.1 |
| 效用原则 | Principle of Utility | An action is good if its benefits exceed its harms; bad if its harms exceed its benefits. | M02 §2.7.1 |
| 最大幸福原则 | Greatest Happiness Principle | An action is right (or wrong) to the extent that it increases (or decreases) the total happiness of the affected parties. | M02 §2.7.1 |
| 后果主义 | Consequentialist theory | A theory holding that the morality of an action depends only on its consequences, not on intent. | M02 §2.7.2 |
| 行为功利主义 | Act Utilitarianism | Applies the Principle of Utility to individual actions: add up the change in happiness of all affected beings; the right action maximizes the sum. | M02 §2.7.2 |
| 快乐计算 | Hedonic Calculus | Bentham's seven dimensions for weighing pleasure: intensity, duration, certainty, propinquity, fecundity, purity, extent. | M02 §2.7.3 |
| 道德运气 | Moral luck | Being assigned moral praise or blame for consequences outside one's control. | M02 §2.7.5 |
| 规则功利主义 | Rule Utilitarianism | We ought to adopt moral rules which, if followed by everyone, will lead to the greatest increase in total happiness. | M02 §2.7.6 |
| 社会契约论 | Social Contract Theory | Morality consists in the set of rules, governing how people are to treat one another, that rational people will agree to accept, for their mutual benefit, on the condition that others follow those rules as well. (Rachels) | M02 §2.8.1 |
| 自然状态 | State of nature | Hobbes's hypothetical condition without a social contract, in which life would be "solitary, poor, nasty, brutish, and short". | M02 §2.8.1 |
| 消极权利 | Negative right | A right that another can guarantee by leaving you alone. | M02 §2.8.2 |
| 积极权利 | Positive right | A right obligating others to do something on your behalf. | M02 §2.8.2 |
| 绝对权利 | Absolute right | A right guaranteed without exception. | M02 §2.8.2 |
| 有限权利 | Limited right | A right that may be restricted based on the circumstances. | M02 §2.8.2 |
| 差异原则 | The difference principle | Any social and economic inequalities must be to the greatest benefit of the least-advantaged members of society. (Rawls) | M02 §2.8.3 |
| 德性伦理 | Virtue Ethics | A right action is an action that a virtuous person, acting in character, would do in the same circumstances. | M02 §2.9.2 |
| 德性 | Virtue (arete, excellence) | A deep-seated character trait, developed by habitually performing right action, that human beings need in order to flourish and be truly happy. | M02 §2.9.2 |
| 恶习 | Vice | A character trait that prevents a human being from flourishing; often a virtue sits between two vices. | M02 §2.9.2 |
| 繁盛 | Flourishing | Living up to one's highest potential; the goal the virtues serve. | M02 §2.9.2 |
| 五常 | Confucian Virtues | 仁 Benevolence, 义 Righteousness, 礼 Propriety, 智 Wisdom, 信 Integrity. | M02 §2.9.4 |

## H · 正义与公平（M03）

| 中文 | English | 英文定义（可默写） | 首现 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 正义 | Justice | Concerned with the rightful allocation of benefits and burdens; rooted in moral philosophy and social order; questions of distribution, recognition, and participation. | [[M03-偏见与公平]] §2.1 |
| 平等 vs 公平 | Equality vs Equity | Equality = sameness, giving everyone the same thing; Equity = fairness, access to the same opportunities. | M03 §2.1 |
| 分配正义 | Distributive Justice | Involves the fair distribution of benefits and burdens; fairness of outcomes. | M03 §2.2 |
| 报应正义 | Retributive Justice | Fairness when blaming or punishing a person for doing wrong. | M03 §2.2 |
| 补偿正义 | Compensatory Justice | The fairness when restoring to a person what the person lost when he or she was wronged by someone else. | M03 §2.2 |
| 程序正义 | Procedural Justice | Fairness of decision-making processes; elements: voice, neutrality, trust, respect. | M03 §2.2, §2.8.3 |
| 互动正义 | Interactional Justice | Quality of interpersonal treatment; informational justice (explanations and communication) + interpersonal justice (courtesy, respect, empathy). | M03 §2.2, §2.8.3 |
| 分配正义的形式原则 | Formal principle of distributive justice | Individuals similar in all relevant respects should be given similar benefits and burdens; individuals dissimilar in a relevant respect ought to be treated dissimilarly in proportion to their dissimilarity. | M03 §2.3 |
| 实质正义原则 | Material principle of justice | A theory of distributive justice specifying the "relevant respects" (contribution, need, freedom, fairness…). | M03 §2.3 |
| 平等主义 / 自由至上主义 | Egalitarianism / Libertarianism | Justice as equality: equal access to resources / Justice as freedom: entitlement and property rights. | M03 §2.3 |
| 正义即公平 | Justice as Fairness (Rawls) | The distribution of benefits and burdens is just if and only if (1) each person has an equal right to the most extensive basic liberties compatible with similar liberties for all; (2) inequalities are (a) to the greatest benefit of the least advantaged and (b) attached to positions open to all under fair equality of opportunity. Principle 1 has priority over 2; 2b over 2a. | M03 §2.4.1 |
| 平等自由原则 | Principle of Equal Liberty | Each citizen's basic liberties must be protected from invasion by others and must be equal to those of others. | M03 §2.4.1 |
| 公平机会平等原则 | Principle of Fair Equality of Opportunity | Everyone should be given an equal opportunity to qualify for the more privileged positions in society's institutions. | M03 §2.4.1 |
| 原初状态 | Original Position | An imaginary meeting of rational self-interested persons who must choose the principles of justice by which their society will be governed. | M03 §2.4.2 |
| 最大最小规则 | Maximin Rule | Prioritizing the least advantaged: choose the option whose worst outcome is best. | M03 §2.4.2 |
| 罗尔斯方法 | Rawls's method | A principle is morally justified if and only if it would be acceptable to rational self-interested persons who do not know their sex, race, abilities, religion, social position or income in that society. | M03 §2.4.3 |
| 可逆性 / 可普遍化 / 人是目的 | Reversibility / Universalizability / Treating people as ends | Parties choose principles they apply to themselves / principles apply equally to everyone / each party has equal say. | M03 §2.4.3 |
| 公平启发式 | Fairness heuristics | Equality heuristic (split evenly), equity heuristic (proportional to contribution), procedural heuristic (process matters more than outcome). | M03 §2.6 |
| 认知偏差 | Cognitive bias | Unconscious and automatic processes designed to make decision-making quicker; a tendency to lean in favor of or against a person, group, idea or thing, usually in an unfair way. | M03 §2.7.1 |
| 自利 / 确认 / 损失厌恶 / 锚定 / 框架 | Self-serving / Confirmation / Loss aversion / Anchoring / Framing bias | Interpreting rules to one's advantage / seeking evidence that supports one's view / negative outcomes weigh more than positive / first impression rules / how you say it matters more than what you say. | M03 §2.7 |
| 前景理论 | Prospect theory | The value function through the reference point is S-shaped and asymmetrical, steeper for losses than gains: losses outweigh gains. | M03 §2.7.2 |
| 调节焦点 | Regulatory focus | Promotion vs. prevention orientation. | M03 §2.8.1 |
| 最后通牒博弈 / 独裁者博弈 | Ultimatum game / Dictator game | Responders reject low offers / altruism vs. selfishness in giving. | M03 §2.8.2 |
| 程序公平效应 | Procedural fairness effect | Perceived fairness of the process (voice, neutrality, trust, respect) affects acceptance independently of the outcome. | M03 §2.8.3 |
| 组织公正 | Organizational justice | Distributive (fair pay, benefits, promotions), procedural (transparent policies), interactional (respectful HR practices), informational (clear, honest communication). | M03 §2.9.1 |

## I · AI 偏见与缓解（M03）

| 中文 | English | 英文定义（可默写） | 首现 |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI 偏见 | AI bias | The occurrence of biased results due to human biases that skew the original training data or AI algorithm—leading to distorted outputs and potentially harmful outcomes. (IBM) | M03 §2.10.2 |
| 偏见 / 公平（技术定义） | Bias / Fairness | Bias: systematic deviation from expected outcomes, often disadvantaging certain groups. Fairness: equitable treatment across individuals or groups. | M03 §2.10.4 |
| 历史 / 代表性 / 测量 / 聚合 / 评估偏见 | Historical / Representation / Measurement / Aggregation / Evaluation bias | Training data reflecting past inequalities / groups underrepresented in datasets / inaccurate quantification of features or outcomes / ignoring group differences by applying models uniformly / using unsuitable benchmarks for testing. | M03 §2.11.3 |
| 数据 / 抽样 / 标签偏见 | Data / Sampling / Label bias | Training data skewed or incomplete / data from non-representative sources / human-labeled data reflecting subjective or biased judgments. | M03 §2.11.4 |
| 代理变量 | Proxy | A flawed stand-in for a real-world trait (e.g., postal code for creditworthiness). | M03 §2.11.4 |
| 红线划定 | Redlining | Mortgage denials based on zip codes. | M03 §2.11.1 |
| 算法偏见（本讲定义） | Algorithmic bias | Model architecture or training process introduces bias; can emerge even with "clean" data. | M03 §2.11.5 |
| 社会偏见 | Societal bias | AI reflects and amplifies existing inequalities. | M03 §2.11.5 |
| 反馈回路 | Feedback loop | Biased outcomes reinforce biased data: Bias → Outcome → Data → Bias. | M03 §2.11.5 |
| 群体公平 / 个体公平 | Group / Individual fairness | Equal outcomes across demographic groups / similar individuals treated similarly. | M03 §2.12.1 |
| 反事实公平 | Counterfactual fairness | A decision is fair if it would remain the same in a counterfactual world where the individual belonged to a different group. | M03 §2.12.2 |
| 差别性影响 | Disparate impact | Outcomes that disproportionately affect protected groups; does not require intent—just unequal results. | M03 §2.12.3 |
| 五分之四规则 | Four-fifths rule | A selection rate for any group below 80% of the highest group's rate is evidence of disparate impact. (🔗 US EEOC guidelines) | M03 §2.12.3 |
| 人口均等 / 机会均等 / 校准 | Demographic parity / Equal opportunity / Calibration | Equal positive outcomes across groups / equal true positive rates / predictions equally accurate across groups. | M03 §2.12.4 |
| 公平–准确率权衡 | Accuracy–fairness trade-off | Improving fairness may reduce predictive accuracy; leaders must weigh ethical vs. operational goals. | M03 §2.12.5 |
| 合成数据 | Synthetic data | Artificially generated data used to fill gaps for underrepresented groups. | M03 §2.13.1 |
| 偏见审计 | Bias audit | Regularly test models for bias across key metrics; third-party audits; document findings and mitigation steps. | M03 §2.13.1 |
| 可解释 AI · 特征重要性 | Explainable AI (XAI) · Feature importance | Makes AI decisions interpretable to humans; feature importance calculates a score for all input features of a model. Tools: SHAP, LIME. | M03 §2.13.2 |
| 人在环路 | Human-in-the-loop | Combining AI with human oversight; humans validate or override AI decisions in high-stakes areas. | M03 §2.13.2 |
| 包容性设计 | Inclusive design | Involving diverse stakeholders and personas from marginalized groups in design and testing. | M03 §2.13.3 |
| 模型卡 / 数据表 | Model card / Datasheet | Documentation of model details, intended use, factors, metrics, evaluation and training data, quantitative analyses, ethical considerations and caveats / the equivalent for a dataset. ⚠️ 与 IS5542 M02 的"模型卡"（厂商一页式模型简介）不是一回事 | M03 §2.13.3 |
| 升级路径 | Escalation path | A defined route for reporting and resolving bias incidents. | M03 §2.14.2 |
| AI 伦理委员会 | AI ethics board | An internal or external advisory group that reviews high-risk models and decisions and provides ethical guidance and accountability. | M03 §2.14.3 |
| 心理安全 | Psychological safety | A team climate in which dissenting views can be voiced without fear. | M03 §2.14.3 |
